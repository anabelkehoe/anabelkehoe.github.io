About Me

I am currently an undergraduate Electrical Engineering student at UC San Diego; graduating December 2027.

My work centers on PCB design for avionics and robotics development. I’m interested in building hardware systems that can be tested, iterated on, and trusted in real conditions. I have experience with PCB design in Altium and kiCAD, and experience integrating with fusion360 and solidworks.

Outside of academics, I’ve participated in chess tournaments and spent 5 years as a competitive gymnast. The photo above is from the Georgia Aquarium in Atlanta!

SEDS @ UCSD

Students for the Exploration and Development of Space (SEDS) at UCSD is a student-run collegiate rocketry organization. SEDS is a leading collegiate rocketry organization pushing the boundaries of student-built aerospace systems, with a history of pioneering liquid propulsion and flight vehicle development.

I currently serve as the Director of Electrical Engineering for the club, and I am a prominent member of the Self Landing Rocket team for the rocket project Tidepool.

I previously worked as avionics lead on an archived Self Landing Rocket: Riptide.
