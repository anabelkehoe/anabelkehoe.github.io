ARCLAB

UCSD ARClab is a medical robotics research lab focused on developing advanced electromechanical systems for surgical and assistive robotic applications. I work with graduate researchers to design, build, and test PCB-based control and sensing hardware, supporting system integration of motors, sensors, and actuators while iterating on designs through hands-on validation and testing.

Often, I am a “floater” in the lab and contribute to the electromechanical integration of different projects.

Projects

Controller Board

Designed a modular motor controller PCB for surgical robotics applications, integrating a microcontroller, motor driver, and configurable I/O for servos, LEDs, buttons, and other peripheral hardware to support multiple use cases on a single platform. The system was initially prototyped on a breadboard for rapid validation, then iterated through multiple redesigns based on lab requirements. Early test versions were developed in KiCad before transitioning to a production-ready design in Altium, allowing for improved routing, robustness, and scalability. This workflow enabled a full hardware development cycle from prototyping to finalized PCB implementation across multiple EDA platforms.
